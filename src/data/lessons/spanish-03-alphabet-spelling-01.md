Welcome to **Spanish Lesson 02**. In this lesson, you will learn the Spanish alphabet, how to ask how a word is spelled, how to spell names, and key pronunciation rules for sounding out Spanish words!

## 4. Sounding Out Words (Pronunciación)

Spanish is a **phonetic language**, meaning words are spoken almost exactly as they are written! Key rules to keep in mind:

### The silent H (La 'H' muda)
The letter **H** is always completely silent in Spanish.

**Hola** (Sounds like *"O-la"*)
<audio controls src="https://translate.google.com/translate_tts?ie=UTF-8&client=tw-ob&tl=es&q=Hola" type="audio/mpeg">
  Your browser does not support the audio element.
</audio>

### The J and G sound (La 'J' y la 'G')
The letter **J** sounds like an English *H* (or a raspy breath).

**Jirafa**
<audio controls src="https://translate.google.com/translate_tts?ie=UTF-8&client=tw-ob&tl=es&q=Jirafa" type="audio/mpeg">
  Your browser does not support the audio element.
</audio>

### The Ñ sound (La 'Ñ' eñe)
**Ñ** makes a "ny" sound, like the middle of *canyon*.

**España**
<audio controls src="https://translate.google.com/translate_tts?ie=UTF-8&client=tw-ob&tl=es&q=Espa%C3%B1a" type="audio/mpeg">
  Your browser does not support the audio element.
</audio>

### The double L sound (La 'LL' doble ele)
**LL** sounds like the *Y* in "yes".
