# Spanish Travel Survival Phrases

Welcome to **Spanish Lesson 03**. When traveling, being able to ask for help, confirm essential details, and handle everyday interactions will make your trip smooth and stress-free. In this lesson, you'll learn key phrases for getting assistance, clarifying directions, and basic travel transactions!

---

## 1. Asking for Help (Pedir ayuda)

When you need immediate assistance or are lost, use these key phrases to get help politely:
